About this series

AI agents are getting more capable, but that capability has not translated into reliability for high-stakes tasks. This blog series explores Constrained Creativity: instead of letting AI act arbitrarily, we give it the least freedom it needs, as a domain-specific language of building blocks it can combine, and expand that space only when necessary.

We write about the ideas, the systems we build with them, and how they compare with related approaches.
